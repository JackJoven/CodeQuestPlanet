# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are 10–14-year-old learners who are new to programming or moving on from block-based coding. The homepage also needs to help parents and educators quickly understand what students will learn and make.

## Product Purpose

CodeQuestPlanet turns programming foundations into an explorable 3D world. Learners solve visible missions, debug with evidence, build original challenges, and publish work for others to play and Remix. Success means a visitor understands the learning journey and confidently starts the CodeQuestPlanet experience.

## Positioning

CodeQuestPlanet is a persistent programming universe where code does not merely answer exercises—it changes a world the learner owns and can share.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: start the free CodeQuestPlanet experience.
- Secondary CTA: inspect the complete learning path.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: 在玩中学编程，在创造中改变世界。
- Belief ladder: this feels like a real game world; every mission teaches a concrete programming skill; the curriculum grows from fundamentals to algorithms and creation; progress produces work worth sharing.
- Proof on hand: the working CodeQuestPlanet prototype, the v1.5 64-lesson curriculum specification, the v1.3 48-lesson implemented course structure, and interface direction files under `界面/`.

## Brand Personality

Adventurous, kinetic, and inventive. The voice is encouraging and direct, giving young learners the feeling of joining a capable expedition rather than sitting through a lesson.

## Anti-references

Avoid school administration portals, generic pale SaaS landing pages, casino-like reward overload, fake partner or learner metrics, and decoration that competes with the learning journey. The interface must not rely on unreadably small text or uncontrolled neon glow.

## Design Principles

1. Show the living world before explaining the curriculum.
2. Make learning progress feel like an expedition with a clear destination.
3. Tie every promise to an existing mission, course structure, or creation outcome.
4. Treat failure and debugging as visible progress, not punishment.
5. Keep the primary action obvious for both young learners and guiding adults.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable Simplified Chinese, keyboard-accessible navigation, strong text contrast, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, touch targets of at least 42 pixels, and layouts that remain understandable without color alone.
